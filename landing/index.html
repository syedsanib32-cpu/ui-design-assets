Questions, answered

How does Cadence get my calls?

Cadence connects to your dialer and meeting tools and ingests calls automatically. There's nothing for reps to upload or remember.

Can I use my own sales methodology?

Yes. Bring your own scorecard or use a built-in framework like MEDDICC or SPICED. Cadence scores every call against it.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Enterprise plans add SSO, role-based access, and regional data residency.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are live within a day. Connect your stack, pick a scorecard, and calls start flowing in immediately.
